#4471 is closed, but not every report in it is solved. The one-day moves have no fix: keep their ticket open and keep sending new one-day reports to it, not to #4471.

The three problems

  • parser, fixed. The shared parser read every date month first, so a day-first tenant’s date came out with day and month swapped (7 April became 4 July). Engineering shipped fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.
  • hint, won’t fix. The booking-v3 form printed MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and read the date day first. Same swap, but only through the v3 form, so the parser fix never touched it. The booking-v3 form is retired, so nothing new comes in this way.
  • one-day, open. No swap at all: the date moves by one day (the invite says Tuesday 12 May, the calendar Monday 11 May), two clients in different time zones get different days for the same meeting, or an invite arrives before the booking was made. That points at time zones, not date order. Neither outcome covers it.

Which problem a report belongs to depends on its tenant, its way in and what it describes. The intake tag does not decide it: it is a guess from the reporter’s browser.

Why they were merged

All three came in under the same subject lines (“Date is wrong”, even “Month and day swapped?” on a report where nothing was swapped). Decision 0001 merges on subject alone, so the subject never told them apart. Only the tenant, the way in and the body do.

Lessons

  • A big ticket can hold more than one bug. Before marking it known, group it by what a fix would have to change, not by subject.
  • Closing the big ticket is not closing its reports. Check each problem against what shipped.
